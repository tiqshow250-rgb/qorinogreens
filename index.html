Who we are

A company built to connect what has always been kept apart.

Qorino Green was founded in October 2026 on a simple observation: the climate, food and biodiversity crises are one crisis wearing six different masks. Labs, farms, forests and emergency agencies each hold a piece of the answer — but they rarely share a language. We build the layer that lets them.

01 — MEASURE

Ground truth first

Satellite imagery, drone surveys, soil labs, bioacoustic sensors and community rangers feed one verified data layer. No claim leaves the platform without an evidence trail behind it.

02 — MODEL

Risk & impact modelling

Cross-sector models connect a gene-edited variety to a drought forecast, or a flood warning to an insurance payout. Every sector improves the others' predictions.

03 — MOBILISE

Capital and people

Projects are matched with funders, machinery, technical partners and policy pathways — released in stages, tied to measured outcomes, not promises.

Biotechnology

Biological tools that replace chemistry — enzymes, microbes, bio-stimulants and bio-based materials.

Problem

Biological solutions take years to travel from a lab bench to a farmer's field, and the cost per unit keeps bio-based inputs out of reach for the smallholders who need them most.

Challenges

  • Scaling fermentation from 2 L to 20,000 L without losing yield
  • Slow, fragmented regulatory approval across countries
  • Biosafety and containment assurance for field trials
  • No shared reference data for soil microbiomes

Our Solution

A shared bio-foundry and distributed field-trial network with open protocols. Labs prototype on the platform, farmers host trials, and results — including failures — return to a public dataset that shortens the next product's path to market.

Machinery & Technologies

  • Benchtop bioreactors
  • Soil metagenomic sequencers
  • IoT biosensors
  • AI protein-folding models
  • CRISPR screening kits
  • Microbiome reference libraries
🌾

Agriculture

Precision, soil health and post-harvest systems that make every hectare produce more with less.

Problem

Yields have plateaued while input costs and climate volatility climb. Farmers are asked to produce more food on degrading soil with less water — and with almost no reliable data about their own land.

Challenges

  • Topsoil loss and falling organic carbon
  • Irrigation demand outstripping supply
  • Fragmented smallholder plots with no digital record
  • Post-harvest losses of 20–40% in many regions

Our Solution

A soil-health passport for every plot, combined with precision-agronomy advisories delivered through cooperatives. Farmers get input plans tuned to their own soil and weather; the platform gets the ground-truth data that makes every other sector's model sharper.

Machinery & Technologies

  • Multispectral survey drones
  • Variable-rate seeders
  • Soil moisture probes
  • Autonomous mechanical weeders
  • Solar cold-chain units
  • Farm management platforms
🧪

Genetic Engineering

Climate-resilient traits — drought, heat, salinity and disease tolerance — developed faster and licensed fairly.

Problem

Conventional breeding takes 8–12 years to deliver a new variety. Climate zones are shifting faster than that. The traits farmers need most are arriving after the climate they were bred for has already changed.

Challenges

  • Long generation cycles in staple crops
  • Public trust and transparent stewardship
  • Concentrated IP ownership blocking small breeders
  • Uneven biosafety regulation between countries

Our Solution

Marker-assisted selection combined with gene editing in a speed-breeding pipeline, run with open stewardship records and a licensing model that guarantees smallholder and public-sector access to climate-resilient varieties.

Machinery & Technologies

  • CRISPR-Cas12a editing platforms
  • SNP genotyping arrays
  • Phenotyping greenhouses
  • Speed-breeding pods
  • Bioinformatics trait pipelines
  • Seed provenance tracking
🛰️

Climate & Disaster Protection

Early warning, rapid assessment and financial protection for the communities hit first and hardest.

Problem

Most communities receive minutes of warning before a flood, cyclone or wildfire — often not at all. Every event compounds the last one, and recovery finance arrives months after the damage.

Challenges

  • Sparse ground sensor coverage in vulnerable regions
  • Warning systems that stop at the district office
  • No last-mile delivery in low-connectivity areas
  • Slow, loss-assessment-heavy insurance payouts

Our Solution

A multi-hazard early-warning layer — radar, satellite and community sensors feeding one model — paired with parametric micro-insurance that pays automatically when a measured threshold is crossed, and pre-agreed response playbooks for local agencies.

Machinery & Technologies

  • Compact weather radar
  • IoT river & rain gauges
  • Sentinel-1 SAR imagery
  • Flood & drought forecast models
  • SMS / radio last-mile alerts
  • Drone damage assessment
🌳

Afforestation

Restoration measured in surviving trees, not planted saplings.

Problem

Billions of trees are planted every year and a large share die within three years. Success is reported as saplings put in the ground, not forest standing a decade later — so failure stays invisible.

Challenges

  • Species planted where they cannot survive
  • No funding for the 3–5 years of aftercare
  • Monitoring stops the day the project closes
  • Unclear land tenure on restoration sites

Our Solution

Survival-linked restoration: seed provenance tracking from locally adapted mother trees, drone-assisted seeding on terrain access, five-year aftercare written into every contract, and independent monitoring that verifies carbon and biodiversity outcomes before payment.

Machinery & Technologies

  • Seed-dispersal drones
  • IoT-climate-controlled nurseries
  • LiDAR canopy scanners
  • Mycorrhizal soil inoculants
  • MRV / carbon verification platforms
  • Community ranger apps
🛡️

Anti-Deforestation

Detect forest loss in days, trace it to a supply chain, and put the evidence in the hands of the people on the ground.

Problem

Forest loss is typically confirmed months after it happens, by which time the timber has moved and the land has been converted. Very little of it is ever traced back to a specific buyer or commodity chain.

Challenges

  • Persistent cloud cover defeating optical satellites
  • Illegal logging that never enters official records
  • Weak or paper-based commodity traceability
  • Communities producing data with no ownership of it

Our Solution

Near-real-time radar monitoring that works through cloud, paired with a trained community ranger network that verifies alerts on the ground, and a commodity traceability ledger so a deforestation alert can be linked to the shipment that followed it.

Machinery & Technologies

  • Sentinel-1 SAR change detection
  • Acoustic logging sensors
  • Blockchain traceability ledger
  • Satellite-linked ranger apps
  • Bioacoustic biodiversity monitors
  • Watchtower camera networks
